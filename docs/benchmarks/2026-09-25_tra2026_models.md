## Reading

- **Best AP: YOLO26s** (mAP50 0.583, mAP50-95 0.465), led by person (+0.09 over the nano
  models) and car. It is 2.5× slower than the nano models here; whether it holds 5 FPS on the
  Pi 5 is untested.
- **Among the nano models, the paper's recipe is best** (mAP50 0.563 vs 0.539 for YOLO26n).
  Our recipe did worse on YOLO11n (0.519): it early-stopped at epoch 67. One seed per run, and
  the delivery_van and truck columns rest on 11 boxes each, so gaps of 0.02–0.03 may be noise.
- **YOLO11n with our recipe counted no people** on the clip. Not a pipeline fault: on the same
  frames it has 131 person detections above 0.5 confidence against 900 for the paper's recipe,
  too few to become confirmed tracks.
- **No model passes S7.** Car and SUV are confused in every model (SUV BA counted 4–10 for 3;
  car BA 4–14 for 6), and no model finds the two delivery vans. The labels of these classes
  are the likely limit (see `docs/labelling_guide/`).
- **e-scooter AP ~0.98 everywhere** is suspect: clips from the same camera share the scene.
  Group the split by camera before trusting it.
- **Nothing promoted.** One 30-crossing clip cannot rank models whose errors differ by a few
  counts; more hand-counted clips are needed before a count-based choice.
